About me

What I specialize in

  • Product Systems
  • Experience Design
  • Generative AI

My work lives at the intersection of systems thinking and visual craft. I care about how information is structured just as much as how it looks: because in complex products, clarity is the design. That's sharpened something for me over time: good design in high-stakes spaces isn't about simplifying complexity, it's about making complexity navigable. I also prototype in code with AI collaboration, which means I can pressure-test ideas at higher fidelity before they ever reach an engineer.
